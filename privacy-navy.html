Privacy

Cookie and privacy settings

We use browser local storage to remember your privacy choice for 180 days. This necessary record stays on your device and is not sent to Utana. You can review it through Cookie settings in the footer, or remove it by clearing this site’s browser data.

Personalisation, statistics, and marketing storage are not currently used. Accept all, Reject optional, and Save preferences therefore save the same necessary-only setting. New tracking services would require a separate choice. Closing the panel without saving leaves optional tracking off and shows the notice again on a later visit.

This website does not set analytics or marketing cookies. The enquiry form prepares a draft in your email application; it does not send or store submissions on this website.

If you send an email, Utana receives the information you include and can use it to respond to your enquiry. Contact fatmah@utana.group with questions about your information.

Fonts load from Google Fonts, which receives the network information needed to serve them. The website host may also process standard access logs. Your email provider handles any draft or message you send through it.
