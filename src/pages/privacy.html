Privacy

This website does not set analytics or marketing cookies. The enquiry form prepares a draft in your email application; it does not send or store submissions on this website.

If you send an email, Utana receives the information you include and can use it to respond to your enquiry. Contact sapiens@utana.group with questions about your information.

Fonts load from Google Fonts, which receives the network information needed to serve them. The website host may also process standard access logs. Your email provider handles any draft or message you send through it.
